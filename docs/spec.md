# fifty5 spec

fifty5 generates new puzzles to solve on a physical IQ Puzzler Pro set. It is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with SmartGames, and uses none of their puzzles, artwork or logo.

## Product

- Companion site: the player solves on the physical board, and the site shows the setup, hints, timer and solution.
- Public static site on GitHub Pages. No accounts or backend; progress is saved only in that browser.
- Designed for a phone lying next to the board. Every piece has its colour and a letter, and a tray shows the pieces still to place.
- English only. Follows the system light or dark setting.

## Pieces and board

12 pieces cover 55 cells on an 11x5 rectangle. Pieces can be rotated and flipped. Reference layout, read from a photo of the set:

```
P P P B B B C Y Y Y Y     P pink     B blue      C light blue  Y yellow
M M P P O B C C Y R R     M mint     O orange    R red         U purple
M M O O O B U U N N R     N navy     L lime      D dark red    T teal
L M L O D D T U U N R
L L L D D T T T U N R
```

The board has 4,331,140 complete solutions, or 1,082,785 ignoring rotations and mirror images.

## Puzzles

A puzzle has some pieces already on the board. The remaining pieces must have exactly one solution, proven by the solver. Rotated or mirrored copies count as duplicates.

| Level | Pieces left | Free play | Reserved for daily (10 years) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | 3-4 | 3,000 | 520 |
| Junior | 5-6 | 3,000 | 520 |
| Expert | 7-8 | 3,000 | 1,040 |
| Master | 9 | 3,000 | 1,040 |
| Wizard | 10 | all the rest | 520 |

- Levels covering two piece counts split evenly between them.
- Solver effort filters out the easiest and hardest 10% within each piece count, except in Wizard.
- Wizard is exactly 10 pieces left and uses every such puzzle (about 4,425). Unique puzzles with 11 or 12 pieces left do not exist.

The bank is compact JSON split by level, committed to the repo and generated by `npm run generate`. Puzzles are only ever added to the end, so links and past dailies never change.

## Play

- **New puzzle:** pick a level and get a random puzzle you haven't solved. Skipped puzzles can come back. Once every puzzle in a level is solved, the level starts again from the full set.
- **Sharing:** each puzzle has a stable link, such as `#wizard-0123` or `#wizard-d0012` for a daily.
- **Hints:** each hint reveals one piece, either the player's choice or the remaining piece with the fewest possible positions. Revealing the full solution is a separate, confirmed button, and a revealed puzzle can't be saved as a solve.
- **Timer:** optional, with Start and Pause, and keeps the screen awake. It keeps running through a locked phone or app switch until Pause or Solved. Leaving a puzzle inside the site pauses it.
- **Reloading:** the puzzle, revealed hints and elapsed time all come back.
- **Saved solves:** tapping Solved saves the puzzle, date, hints used and time if tracked, with a "save without time" option. Best times count only no-hint solves.
- **Puzzle of the day:** one a day, changing at local midnight, from a separate pool. Mon Starter, Tue Junior, Wed Expert, Thu Master, Fri Wizard, Sat Expert, Sun Master. There is no archive; a missed daily can only be reached through a shared link.

## Build

- TypeScript and Vite, with one solver shared by the site and the generator script. Deploys through GitHub Actions.
- The solver works on generic cells and placements, so other boards only add data.
- Tests check that every puzzle in the bank has exactly one solution.

## Not in v1

The other two boards (including the 3D pyramid), on-screen play, accounts and sync, offline install, streaks and leaderboards, and moving history between devices.
